Tracks supplied by Max for the game, preserved as the original MP3 bytes.

| File | Original download | Credit | Situation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `space-ambient.mp3` | `leberch-space-ambient-509783.mp3` | Music by Nikita Kondrashev | Calm |
| `interstellar-space.mp3` | `sergepavkinmusic-interstellar-space-167196.mp3` | Music by Sergii Pavkin | Moderate tension |
| `rising-dread.mp3` | `alex-morgan-tense-suspense-rising-dread-573934.mp3` | Music by Alex Morgan | Imminent battle |

Artist credits appear in **Manual → Music credits**. Download filenames and credits were supplied by Max; no license document accompanied these files.

Music uses the live player's visible haulers and sites, including outbound attacks, inbound attacks, defended neutral sites, interceptions, and passing-hauler battles. Known battles within six game hours raise moderate tension; within one game hour they raise imminent tension. Friendly traffic, gifts active by contact, unseen conflicts, and empty neutral expansion don't raise tension. Completed operations return to calm. Forecasting and replay scrubbing don't select the soundtrack.

Tracks loop at 24% volume with two-second crossfades. The shared Sound switch mutes music and effects. Music players are released on mute, leaving the operation, or entering the background.
