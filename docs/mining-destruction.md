# Mine losses and destroyed outposts

New operations carry `rules.mechanicsVersion: 2`. The outer game/save format is unchanged. Operations without that field retain the previous mechanics, including fractional mine losses and cleared wreck ownership, so their command histories still reproduce the saved state. Loading doesn't change a running game's rules. A new operation uses the corrected behavior.

## Reference contract

The [official rulebook](https://subterfuge-game.com/rulebook/) makes mine capture cost the former owner 20% of their cargo; the conqueror receives no cargo. The [official Martyr reference](https://subterfuge-game.com/rulebook/Specialists.html) specifies an immediate area destruction effect.

The [firsthand 2016 FAQ](https://herbertzhb.wordpress.com/2016/06/28/subterfuge-2/) supplies details omitted by the official summaries: round the lost cargo upward; retain a destroyed outpost's owner and sonar; exclude wrecks from mining territory; and charge no mine-loss penalty when ownership hasn't changed. These details are the implemented interpretation. That FAQ contains other rules that conflict with the current official reference, so it isn't treated as authoritative for the whole game.

A capture leaving 11 cargo therefore leaves 8, not 8.8. Capturing a mine resets its mining clock; the attacker gains only the outpost and future production. Repeated captures apply the rounded loss to the remaining balance. Destruction retains the owner's entire balance unless the same event also eliminates that player through core loss.

A wreck retains its location, name, ownership, and sonar. Its drones, specialists, shields, and module are destroyed. It supplies no factory production, generator capacity, mining, or active shield recharge and cannot send or receive new haulers, gifts, or scheduled transfers. Already traveling haulers that later reach a wreck return to friendly territory through the existing arrival policy. Wrecks are excluded from mining-rate territory. Their retained ownership still contributes to the current literal ownership-based domination count; reference acceptance for that count remains open.

Economy scheduling excludes wrecks, and saves permit their frozen past clocks while rejecting surviving wreck resources. UI inspection identifies a destroyed site, explains retained sonar, removes mine/shield controls, and disables transfer/gift selection. A distant wreck doesn't advertise an active mine outside sonar.

## Verification

Engine scenarios cover rounded boundaries, capture versus destruction, retained sonar, lost generator capacity and shields, future inertness, frozen-clock persistence, rejected wreck commands, and invalid wreck data. Server scenarios cover both outcomes through scoped observations, idempotent commands, forecast, historical reconstruction, restart, and later polling.

`src/game/fixtures/mine-replays-e92b028.json` contains synthetic capture and detonation saves generated by the engine at `e92b028`, before this correction. Both must load to their exact recorded states. The prior 24-hour planar fixture remains in the suite. No player data is included.

The integrated suite passes 242 tests; TypeScript, web export, and the standalone production server checks pass. Real browser and phone acceptance of the wreck controls is incomplete: the current Safari binding returns `cgWindowNotFound`. Prior wrapping gesture/translated-copy acceptance also remains outstanding. Specialist interactions outside this correction are tracked in the [independent acceptance audit](specialist-acceptance-audit.md) and issue #18.
