## Reference rules

The publisher's [v42.1 patch](https://www.secretplan.com/subterfuge-news/rules-v42-1.html) (September 17, 2026) and [v42.2 patch](https://www.secretplan.com/subterfuge-news/rules-v42-2.html) (October 12, 2026) amend the older rulebook for newly created games. They establish four previously missed requirements, tracked in [#46](https://github.com/mcclowes/machination/issues/46):

| Amendment | Rebuild status |
| --- | --- |
| Engineer recovery cannot exceed combat losses. | New mechanics revision 7 caps recovery. Station attackers, defenders, and opposing-route winners are covered; converted drones aren't discarded by the repair cap. Old revisions retain their results. |
| Gifts respect the recipient's power capacity. | New mechanics revision 8 discards excess gifted drones at station and sub contact. Existing drones and transferred specialists survive; older revisions retain uncapped gifts. |
| The opening 12 game hours accelerate sub movement, Queen recruitment, factories, and shield recharge by 1.5x. | New mechanics revision 9 uses a shared progress clock across travel, recruitment, factories, shields, forecasts, and observed replays. Mines, preparation, station abilities, and asteroid drift keep actual-hour timing. |
| Navigator course changes take effect after ten minutes; the eight-hour cooldown remains. | New mechanics revision 10 keeps the current route during a private, cancelable pending order, then turns from the position reached at its deadline. Older revisions retain immediate redirection. |

Revision 7 also records the drifting-base variant explicitly, independently of mechanics version. Existing revision-six games retain their implicit drifting rules. Save loading, canonical replay, public shared replay, room retries, and setup controls cover both old and new profiles. These checks establish compatibility, not complete acceptance of the two publisher patches or original-client UI parity.

Gift-capacity acceptance covers exact/over-cap contacts, specialist-only survivors, empty drone-only gifts, friendly transfers, reciprocal submission order, capacity-granting specialists, old rules, saves, canonical replay, and authoritative retries/history/restart. The recipient's capacity is evaluated after the specialists transfer and leadership settles; reciprocal outgoing manifests are both removed before either incoming manifest is capped. These two ordering choices are consistent rebuild contracts, but the patch does not specify their original-client ordering. Client comparison remains open.

Opening acceptance checks departure before/at noon, moving-base rendezvous, opposing-route combat, Pirate interception/return, preparation edits, Tycoon changes, damage/recharge, recruitment, unaffected mine/ability timers, old clocks, saved state, and partitioned advancement. Initial factory cycles finish at 5h 20m, 10h 40m, and 18h; initial recruitment credits arrive at 2h 40m and 16h. The patch's factory-cycle reduction is interpreted as exactly two-thirds duration, consistent with its stated 1.5x speed. Private history and the shared replay's movement/visibility geometry use the same piecewise clock; service restart at hour 12 retains the course and recruitment deadline. Time controls mark the opening boundary, and recruitment uses the shared calendar display. Revision 10 includes these opening rules; existing revisions retain their clocks.

The four contested matches also pass under revision 9 with unchanged contest assertions and controllers checking their own observations every eight actual hours. Earlier twelve-hour controller results are historical evidence; current acceptance uses eight-hour checks. This is a bounded test policy, not an original-game AI comparison. Browser UI inspection of the new timing text is not verified because the computer-use bridge returned `cgWindowNotFound`; successful export does not establish rendered or native acceptance.

Navigator acceptance covers continued travel before activation, exact cancellation boundaries, scheduled cancellation, private observations and forecasts, public replay projection, canonical replay, strict saves, destroyed destinations, arrivals during the window, forced Saboteur returns, retirement, and revision-nine immediate behavior. The authoritative service also checks private history, foreign authorization, retries, cancellation, and disk restart. The order drawer shows the pending destination/deadline and cancellation; its React-host check establishes control behavior, not rendered native acceptance.

The cooldown is anchored to physical turns: canceled requests consume no cooldown, and a later order can prepare during the last ten minutes if its eventual turn is at least eight hours after the previous one. Execution validates the active Navigator, ownership, course lock, and destination again. Same-hour arrivals and combat settle before pending turns. These cooldown and tie-order details are explicit rebuild interpretations; the patch does not specify them. Original-client differential checks remain open in #46. Fresh games now use revision 13, retaining these rules, revision-eleven immediate global-effect reconciliation after captive release, and revision-twelve ordinary stationary-route eligibility. Saved games are not upgraded. The [official rulebook](https://subterfuge-game.com/rulebook/) defines the baseline:

| System | Reference contract |
| --- | --- |
| Start | Five outposts each; four carry 40 drillers, one a Queen; dormant outposts undefended; no initial mines. |
| Economy | Factories: six drillers per eight hours. Capacity: 150 plus 50 per generator. |
| Defense | Shields: 10/20 maximum, 48-hour recharge; can be disabled. |
| Orders | Ten-minute editable launch preparation; opposing-route encounters. |
| Combat | Specialist, shield, driller, capture phases; outpost wins equal ties. |
| Mining | Mine cost: 50, 100, 200, 300…; daily output scales with owned outposts; losing one removes 20% of reserves. |
| Victory | 200 mined units, last survivor, or configurable domination target. |
| Sensors | Outpost coverage; incoming and owned subs visible; mine locations public. |
| Gifts | Ownership transfers on contact; ten-minute cancellation. |
| Funding | Twenty-unit lead enables free support: +50 capacity, +2 factory output. |
| Elimination | Queen loss, resignation, or inactivity; assets persist, mining and orders stop. |
| Recruitment | First hire at hour four, then every 18 hours; promotion replaces hiring. |

The [official specialist reference](https://subterfuge-game.com/rulebook/Specialists.html) is the ability specification. Machination's four drone-built modules aren't substitutes for the roster. Multiple transported units, movable leadership, promotions, captives, local/global effects, and ability priorities need explicit support. Reference examples include double-speed transport, conversion rounded upward, interception with accelerated return, and area destruction. Names and descriptions remain original.

## Interaction baseline

The [official Time Machine tutorial](https://vimeo.com/114210812), linked from the [game website](https://subterfuge-game.com/), is the primary UI reference. The developer's [Steam description](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4831650/Subterfuge/) also describes inspection of visible future movement/combat/production and issuing future orders in that context. UI details require visual verification; written descriptions alone don't verify gesture parity.

Acceptance scenarios:

- The chart is the primary play surface on a phone. Pan and zoom without leaving it, select a rock or moving hauler, and inspect its details through contextual controls.
- Ownership, inventories, site types, defenses, modules, sensor boundaries, routes, destination, arrival time, and selected state are legible. Hidden information remains unknown.
- Choose a departure and destination on the map; adjust the manifest, inspect arrival/combat, then confirm. Preparations and queued orders can be reviewed and canceled.
- Move through time precisely, return to live time, inspect known outcomes, and create future orders using the same actions. A forecast doesn't imply knowledge of new human orders or hidden forces.
- Replay observed history, including battles missed while away. History never reveals information the player couldn't observe.
- Reach private and group diplomacy, standings, specialist management, events, and game settings from persistent navigation. Connection/recovery settings don't displace the active chart.
- Preserve accessible controls and large touch targets. Validate keyboard handling, safe areas, and phone layouts as well as desktop export.
