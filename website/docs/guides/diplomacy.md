---
title: Diplomacy and joining an operation
description: Join a multiplayer operation, negotiate, send gifts, and preserve access.
---

Haulers can take hours to reach a border. Talk before you commit the forces that currently defend it.

## Join an invited operation

Open **Multiplayer uplink** with the service address and invitation supplied by your host. The host chooses 2–10 players and a victory mode, then starts after every configured seat has joined.

Public signup and a public browser frontend aren't prerequisites for an invited native playtest. Use the supplied client; this guide doesn't create multiplayer accounts or seats. Follow the login or recovery flow shown by that build.

Before starting, agree on the mode and expected check-in cadence. Multiplayer automatically resigns a player after 48 hours without activity. Opening the operation in the foreground, issuing orders, or chatting renews activity; background refreshes don't.

## Make an agreement explicit

Use private signals for a bilateral conversation, a private group for named members, or the public channel for everyone in the operation.

A useful border agreement names the sites involved, who can move through, and when the agreement expires. Alliances are promises between people. King's Pulse has no ally immunity: your pulse can destroy an ally's assets.

## Send a gift

Review a gift at launch, or turn one of your traveling haulers into a gift. The gift order has a ten-minute cancellation window. Ownership remains yours until contact.

The current owner of the destination receives the manifest without ordinary combat. A passing hostile hauler can accept the gift first. Drones exceeding the recipient's drone capacity are discarded in new operations; specialists still transfer.

For a negotiated site handover, coordinate its timing and [shield settings](../reference/rules.md#production-and-time). A promise in chat doesn't automatically change defenses or ownership.

## Fund another intelligence

In **Intel**, funding becomes available when you lead another intelligence by at least 20 cargo. It costs you nothing and gives the recipient 50 capacity plus two additional drones per factory cycle. It ends if the cargo lead drops below 20.

Sponsor counts are public. Sponsor identities are visible only when you're involved. Spending material on a King's Pulse charge can change whether the funding threshold still holds.

## Share an object at a specific time

Inspect an observed site or hauler in live state or history, choose **Copy site reference** or **Copy hauler reference**, and paste it into chat.

**Inspect** opens the copied time; **Inspect now** uses current observations. The recipient sees only what their own information permits at that time. Forwarding a reference doesn't grant sensor access, and forecasts can't be copied as observed contacts.

## Keep access to your seat

Use the same account or installed client as directed by your build. If the operation offers a recovery code, generate one while connected and save it privately with the service address.

A recovery code is single-use. Restoring the seat disconnects the old device; generate a fresh code after restoration. Never paste a recovery code into game chat. If a command's result is uncertain, retry it rather than recreating the action.
