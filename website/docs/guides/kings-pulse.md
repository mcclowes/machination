---
title: Survive King's Pulse
description: Charge and fire your King's Pulse, understand shields, and use a Disruptor.
---

New operations default to **King's Pulse**. Your movable intelligence core is your King. The last surviving King wins; simultaneous final deaths produce a draw. A fallback core cannot succeed your King in this mode.

The specialist named **Overclock** is a different unit. Its Subterfuge counterpart is called King, which can make the specialist reference confusing at first.

## Build the material reserve

Capture territory and drill mines to produce **pulse material**, the mode's name for mined cargo. Check the [economy rules](../reference/rules.md#production-and-time) before converting a factory or reactor into a mine.

Charge requires 100 material. Committing it spends the reserve without a refund. This can also change funding eligibility.

## Charge while stationed

With your King at a site and enough material available, use its pulse controls to charge. The default charge takes six game hours; opening acceleration doesn't shorten it. An ordinary real-time multiplayer hour is a game hour, while solo clock advancement can move game time faster.

Executing a movement order cancels charging, readiness, or emission immediately, including launch preparation. Canceling that movement afterward doesn't restore the spent material or charge.

## Fire when ready

Firing is manual. The circular wave expands from the firing position and remains dangerous behind its leading edge while your King emits.

Your own assets are immune. Every other intelligence's assets can be hit, including allies. Unprotected enemy sites, traveling haulers, specialists, and Kings can be destroyed.

Moving, losing the King, or ending emission removes its strategic pulse everywhere. There's no posthumous strategic pulse. Check rival defenses and arrival estimates before you fire; they assume current speeds and positions.

## Read pulse shields separately from combat shields

A site's pulse reserve is separate from the shield points that absorb drones in ordinary combat. With the default balance:

| Reserve behavior | Rate |
| --- | --- |
| Full pulse shield | 100 energy |
| Drain from one hostile pulse | 20 energy per game hour |
| Recharge with no pulse present | 5 energy per game hour |

A full reserve holds one continuous hostile pulse for five hours. Overlapping pulses drain faster. Shield controls disable both defenses. Stationed units and preparing haulers receive local protection; ships passing nearby don't.

The shield protects the site without blocking the wave from reaching places beyond it. Inspect exposure and estimated depletion rather than assuming a shielded neighbor shelters you.

## Use a Disruptor for a tactical interruption

In new pulse operations, a [Disruptor](../reference/specialists.md#disruptor) spends 10–25 material and charges for 30 game minutes while stationed. It fires automatically. Movement, capture, or destruction cancels charging without a refund.

Its finite wave weakens with distance and never directly destroys assets or Kings. Strong hits disable engines and mining for an hour, drain 25 pulse shield energy, and reset a charging King. Weaker hits halve local sensor range for an hour. A firing King's wave slows by half for an hour rather than ending. An affected King resists further charge resets and wave slowdowns for six hours.

Unlike a King's strategic pulse, a launched tactical wave survives the Disruptor that emitted it.

## Check your operation's balance

These are the default values for new operations. Hosts can configure pulse balance, and saved operations retain their rules. Use the values displayed in your operation when planning an exact charge, shield collapse, or arrival.
