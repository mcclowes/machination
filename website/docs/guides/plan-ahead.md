---
title: Plan ahead with the Time Machine
description: Inspect arrivals, schedule future orders, and understand forecast uncertainty.
---

The Time Machine lets you inspect history and possible futures without moving the live clock. Use it before committing drones or leaving an operation unattended.

## Check an arrival

1. Select a hauler and inspect its arrival.
2. Check the target's owner, shields, defenders, and specialists at that time.
3. Look for other observed haulers arriving before or after yours.
4. Return to **Now** to see live state.

Forecasts include known orders and your private plans. Hidden garrisons, unseen haulers, future hires, and new rival orders can change the outcome. A visible owner doesn't imply a known defense.

:::warning A forecast is conditional
An uncertain encounter is still uncertain when you scrub forward. Don't treat missing information as an empty garrison or a guaranteed capture.
:::

## Schedule a transfer using future production

Inspect a future time, up to seven days ahead. Select your departure site and plan the transfer from that future context. Confirm the execution time before submitting.

Scheduled orders **don't reserve drones, specialists, credits, or cargo**. They check ownership and resources when they execute. If the full manifest isn't available then, the order aborts.

For example, a future factory cycle may supply the drones you intend to launch. A second order spending those same drones first can make the launch fail. [Borrow tomorrow](../learn/practice.md#borrow-tomorrow) is a safe place to practice this.

## Review your queue

The scheduled-order queue shares the bottom tray with the time controls. It shows your actual live plans even while you're inspecting a forecast. Hide the queue to give the map more space.

- Select an order row to inspect its execution time and reveal its details.
- Edit the selected launch's manifest and choose **Save launch manifest**. Its time, route, and dependent orders stay the same.
- Use the **×** beside a row to cancel it before execution. A processed order can't be undone.

A scheduled launch still prepares for ten minutes when it executes. Choose its launch time with that delay in mind.

## Schedule other actions

The future context also supports recruitment, promotions, captive releases, mine drilling, shield changes, funding, and eligible hauler orders. Buttons say **Schedule** at the selected time.

Requirements still apply at execution: a specialist must be available, an interception must remain reachable, and a navigator must be off cooldown. New recruitment offers beyond the known offer remain unknown until they become available.

## Look backward without changing orders

Scrub into available history or enter a negative offset to inspect a past state. Historical views use what your sensors could see then and are read-only. Return to **Now** before issuing a live command.

Use [map references in diplomacy](diplomacy.md#share-an-object-at-a-specific-time) when another player needs to inspect the same contact and time.
