These examples use site combat without specialists or other modifiers. Read the manifest as well as the drone count before applying the arithmetic to a live encounter.

## An undefended foothold

A site has four defending drones and no shield. Send ten attacking drones.

**10 attackers − 4 defenders = 6 surviving attackers.** You capture the site with six drones.

Send four instead and both drone forces are exhausted. With equal surviving specialists, the defending site keeps its owner.

## A shielded garrison

A site has ten charged shield points and eight defenders. Send 20 drones.

| Phase | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Specialist abilities | None in this example. |
| Shields | Ten attackers are destroyed; the shield drains to zero. |
| Drones | Ten attackers fight eight defenders, leaving two attackers. |
| Capture | You take the site with two surviving drones. |

Sending 18 leaves no attacking drones after shields and defenders. With no specialist advantage, the defender keeps the site. Sending 19 leaves one surviving attacker.

## Arriving later changes the numbers

An inspected garrison can produce more drones or recharge its shields before your hauler arrives. Another fleet can reinforce it first. Inspect the **arrival time** in the [Time Machine](../guides/plan-ahead.md), then allow for hidden information and new rival orders.

## When specialists are involved

The simple subtraction stops being enough. A [Shield breaker](specialists.md#infiltrator) drains defending shields; a [Conversion virus](specialists.md#thief) changes the drone counts; a [Self-destruct charge](specialists.md#martyr) can destroy the whole encounter area.

Local specialist abilities use combat priority, lower first and equal priorities together. The self-destruct effect precedes suppression. A Precedence kernel on only one side suppresses other local combat abilities; kernels on both sides allow them again. Global effects can still apply.

If combat continues, global combat damage applies before shields and drone combat. Surviving active local specialists break drone ties. With equal specialists, a defending site keeps control. Captured specialists become inactive captives. Gift contacts transfer without combat abilities.

Use the [specialist reference](specialists.md) and the in-game combat prediction to inspect a particular encounter. An uncertain prediction remains conditional on what you can see.

## Hauler encounters

Opposing ordinary haulers can meet while traveling opposite legs of the same route. There is no site shield phase in that encounter. Surviving ordinary haulers continue their journey; targeted Scavenger interceptions follow their own return mission.

Pulse shields are a separate defense against [King's Pulse](../guides/kings-pulse.md). Drone combat shield points don't tell you how long a site can withstand a strategic pulse.
