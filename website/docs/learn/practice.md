Choose **Start tactical practice** on the opening screen. Each exercise uses the real chart and orders with a manual clock. Retry starts from the same position; completed exercises keep their marks separately from your live operations.

The exercises teach orders and combat in a mining-mode scenario. They don't teach every rule of the default King's Pulse mode.

## Mission sequence

The main sequence follows the 24 [Subterfuge tutorial missions](https://subterfuge.fandom.com/wiki/Puzzles), using Machination's names, maps, and controls. Four additional exercises follow it. Each mission includes optional hints and checks your actual orders and results.

Original walkthroughs can use historical specialist abilities. Promotion and the advanced exercises adapt those rules to the current catalog, and the fastest-route deadline is shorter on the smaller map. The mine-defense endgame uses the classic 200-cargo quota and free hiring; the other mining endgame uses the current quota.

**Resolve orders** also works after construction, funding, recruitment, promotion, shield changes, and captive release. You can play economic outcomes through time without launching a hauler. A retention objective fails as soon as its defended site changes hands, even if your own core remains safe.

## Open a foothold

**Objective:** capture Quiet landing with surviving drones.

Use Foundry's drones to take the neighboring site. Follow [your first 10 minutes](first-ten-minutes.md) for a complete walkthrough.

<details>
<summary>Hint: how many drones?</summary>

Quiet landing has four defenders. Send more than four drones and allow for ten minutes of preparation before transit.

</details>

## Break the perimeter

**Objective:** overcome shields and defenders, with drones left over.

Read the target's shields as well as its garrison. Shields destroy attacking drones before the defenders fight.

<details>
<summary>Hint: what must the attack overcome?</summary>

Ten shield points plus eight defending drones require more than 18 attacking drones when neither side has a specialist. Try 20, then inspect the arrival. See [the worked combat examples](../reference/combat.md).

</details>

## A quiet passenger

**Objective:** move the thruster alone from Core berth to Foundry, keeping the core at home.

Load the designated specialist explicitly. A specialist can travel with zero drones; it doesn't need an escort just to make a legal transfer.

<details>
<summary>Hint: check the manifest</summary>

Select only the thruster, set drones to zero, and confirm the intelligence core is not selected. Advance the actual exercise until the thruster arrives at Foundry.

</details>

## Borrow tomorrow

**Objective:** before hour 8, queue Foundry's first six drones to launch at hour 8 and capture Quiet landing by hour 10.

The factory starts empty. [Future orders](../guides/plan-ahead.md) can use drones that will exist at execution, so you don't need to wait for production before planning.

<details>
<summary>Hint: use future production</summary>

Inspect hour 8 in the Time Machine and plan a six-drone transfer from Foundry to Quiet landing at that time. Queue it before the live clock reaches hour 8. Production resolves before the launch at the same time. Return to Now and advance the actual exercise through the capture.

</details>

## Cut the approach

**Objective:** intercept the raid before it reaches Core berth, keeping the core safe.

Foundry has an [Interceptor](../reference/specialists.md#pirate). Use the **Interceptor targets** target list to intercept the observed incoming hauler. An ordinary transfer toward a site won't satisfy this objective.

<details>
<summary>Hint: compare contact times</summary>

Include the interceptor and enough drones to defeat the raid. Check that the interception happens before the raid's original arrival, then let the actual interception resolve. The interceptor's speed bonus applies to targeted interception, not every journey it takes.

</details>

## When a forecast looks right but the exercise hasn't passed

Return to **Now**, confirm the order exists in your live queue, then advance the practice clock. Success checks the actual order and resulting game state. A different destination, an ordinary transfer instead of an interception, or moving the core in **A quiet passenger** can fail the objective even if the chart looks favorable.
