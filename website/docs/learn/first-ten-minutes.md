---
title: Your first 10 minutes
description: Capture your first foothold in a safe Machination practice exercise.
---

Start with a small capture in **Tactical practice**. You'll use the same chart, haulers, and orders as an ordinary operation, with a clock you control.

You need a running copy of Machination. If you're joining an invited playtest, use the build supplied by your host. Developers can [run the game from source](https://github.com/mcclowes/machination#run). This website is a guide; it doesn't launch an operation.

## 1. Open the first exercise

Choose **Start tactical practice**, then **Start: Open a foothold**. If you're already inside an operation, return to **Games** first.

Find three places on the chart:

- **Core berth** holds your intelligence core. Keep it safe.
- **Foundry** holds the drones you'll send.
- **Quiet landing** is the foothold you'll capture.

Drag to pan. Pinch or scroll to change scale, or open **Map controls** for zoom, **Focus your territory**, and **Fit chart**. New charts wrap across every edge.

## 2. Plan a transfer

Select **Foundry**, choose **Plan transfer**, then select **Quiet landing** on the chart.

Set the drone manifest to **10**. Leave the intelligence core and other specialists behind. Check that the departure and destination are correct, then launch.

Quiet landing has four defenders in this exercise. With no shields or specialists involved, ten attacking drones defeat four defenders and leave six survivors. Sending four would leave the defender in control. [Combat examples](../reference/combat.md) explain the next cases.

## 3. Check the order before departure

Launches prepare for **10 game minutes**. Inspect your order in **Your live hauler orders**. You can edit or cancel while it prepares.

Once the hauler departs, an ordinary transfer is committed to its destination. Don't assume you can recall it.

## 4. Inspect the arrival

Select the hauler and inspect its arrival in the **Time Machine**. The forecast shows a possible future without advancing the actual exercise.

Check that Quiet landing becomes yours with drones left over. Return to **Now** afterward.

:::tip Forecasting doesn't complete an exercise
You must let your actual order arrive. Looking at a successful forecast alone doesn't pass the objective.
:::

## 5. Let the order resolve

Use the practice clock or **Resolve orders** to advance the actual exercise until your hauler arrives. The exercise stops when you complete its objective.

Quiet landing is now yours. Your completion mark is stored separately from solo and multiplayer progress. **Retry exercise** recreates the same exercise if you'd like to try a smaller manifest.

## Before a live operation

Practice pauses until you advance its clock. **A live operation continues while you're away.** Multiplayer uses server time, and human rivals can change their orders.

Check the victory mode before you start:

| Mode | Your objective |
| --- | --- |
| King's Pulse, the default | Keep your King alive and eliminate every opposing King. Mining funds your pulse. |
| Mining | Reach the operation's displayed cargo target, or become the last active intelligence. |
| Domination | Control half the chart, or become the last active intelligence. |
